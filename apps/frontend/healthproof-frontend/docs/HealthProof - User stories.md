# <a name="_77m60wmv1j8z"></a>**1. Medical Centers**
## <a name="_rvgtidw9yx7b"></a>**Persona**
**Clinical Operations Director / Hospital IT Lead**

Responsible for ensuring medical workflows run smoothly while complying with new interoperability and data protection regulations.

They oversee:

- Electronic medical record systems
- laboratory integrations
- clinical documentation workflows
- regulatory compliance

They are under pressure to **reduce operational risk and ensure data integrity**.

-----
# <a name="_epvlqq3p7fv7"></a>**Core User Stories**
### <a name="_fm5vst3e7b0"></a>**Medical Order Issuance**
As a **doctor**,\
` `I want to **issue verifiable medical test orders**,\
` `so that **laboratories can trust and process them without manual validation**.

-----
### <a name="_s64uo5vm5en2"></a>**Secure Result Verification**
As a **doctor**,\
` `I want to **instantly verify that a laboratory result has not been altered**,\
` `so that **I can make clinical decisions with confidence**.

-----
### <a name="_19379mrr44dm"></a>**Interoperable Patient Care**
As a **medical center**,\
` `I want **patients to move between institutions while maintaining document authenticity**,\
` `so that **clinical workflows do not break across providers**.

-----
### <a name="_y3y2jfzhax6y"></a>**Regulatory Compliance**
As a **hospital administrator**,\
` `I want **every clinical document to have a verifiable audit trail**,\
` `so that **our institution can comply with interoperability and data protection regulations**.

-----
### <a name="_hq0tmw4q9mi0"></a>**Risk Reduction**
As a **healthcare provider**,\
` `I want **proof of document integrity**,\
` `so that **we reduce legal and operational risk from fraudulent or altered records**.

-----
# <a name="_2g1c7klmc3pu"></a>**Jobs-to-be-Done**
Medical centers hire HealthProof to:

• Prove authenticity of clinical documents\
` `• Reduce administrative validation workload\
` `• Enable cross-institution workflows\

` `• Protect against regulatory risk

# <a name="_tv1q2f8x20j"></a>**2. Laboratories**

Responsible for managing diagnostic testing workflows and ensuring that test results are trusted by physicians and institutions.

Their priority is **operational efficiency and credibility of results**.

-----

As a **laboratory technician**,\
` `I want to **verify that a test order was legitimately issued by a doctor**,\
` `so that **we avoid processing fraudulent or altered requests**.

-----
### <a name="_d2kr6i1lcez2"></a>**Secure Result Submission**

` `I want to **attach results to the original medical order**,\
` `so that **the physician can verify their authenticity instantly**.

-----
### <a name="_j5jrz1yan8ll"></a>**Cross-Institution Results**
As a **lab**,\
` `I want **multiple medical centers to trust our results without manual verification**,\
` `so that **our reports can move across healthcare networks seamlessly**.

-----
### <a name="_uynsazt7sap2"></a>**Operational Efficiency**
As a **lab operations manager**,\
` `I want **less administrative validation**,\
` `so that **our staff can focus on diagnostics instead of paperwork**.

-----
